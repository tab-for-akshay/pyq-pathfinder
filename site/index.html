            About this project

            PYQ Pathfinder helps UPSC Indian Forest Service and Civil Services aspirants with Botany and Agriculture optionals spend their limited hours where the exam actually puts its marks.

            What it does

            • Pattern map: 5,794 previous-year question parts (IFoS and CSE Mains, 2001 to 2025, gaps listed) classified into 49 themes and ranked by marks. In Botany, six themes carry over half the marks.
            • Adaptive tutor: 50 MCQs across Botany and Agriculture, each tied to the exam years its theme was asked and each key checked against a cited source. Difficulty climbs one level per right answer; weak themes come back sooner.
            • Hybrid AI: explanations and outlines prefer Gemini Nano on the device; answer grading prefers a cloud Gemini model through Firebase AI Logic and queues while offline. Every AI reply is labelled with where it ran, and verified keys always outrank AI text.
            • Offline-first: after the first visit the app and question bank work with no network.

            How the answer keys were checked

            Each MCQ's key was checked against the page it cites (shown under every answer). An independent human review of all keys is the next step; until then treat a key that looks wrong as a bug and check the cited page.

            Who built it

            Team lead: Akshay Wagh, an IFoS aspirant, who set the goal and reviews the work. Builder: Tab, an AI assistant, which wrote the code, built the dataset and wrote the MCQs. Question sources: UPSC papers via the Wayback Machine, iasexamportal.com, civilserviceindia.com and others.
